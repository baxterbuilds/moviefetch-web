Good to know

Torrent downloads only reach people whose apps can talk to browsers, so less common files may be slow or stall. Free sources (Internet Archive and similar) download straight and fast.

Keep Aloha open while downloading. The background switch plays silence so iOS is less likely to pause the page, but iOS can still stop it — it is not a guarantee.

Very large files can run out of memory on iPhone. 720p is the safest pick for movies.
